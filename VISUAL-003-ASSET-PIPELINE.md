# MA'KYREN VISUAL-003 — Realistic Asset Pipeline

## Hard visual requirements
- No LEGO/voxel/block-person aesthetic.
- Realistic human proportions and silhouettes.
- PBR materials, normal/roughness workflows where available.
- Detailed vehicles with recognizable automotive proportions.
- Dense urban dressing: storefronts, signs, utility poles, street furniture, vegetation, trash, barriers and road markings.
- Mobile-first LODs, texture budgets, instancing and baked/static lighting where practical.

## Free asset sources selected
- Kenney Car Kit — CC0: https://www.kenney.nl/assets/car-kit
- Kenney City Kit (Roads) — CC0: https://www.kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads
- Kenney City Kit (Commercial) — CC0: https://www.kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial
- Kenney City Kit (Suburban) — CC0: https://www.kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban
- Kenney Animated Characters Protagonists — CC0: https://www.kenney.nl/assets/animated-characters-protagonists

These are free/CC0 sources; they are suitable as production building blocks, but the final character identity and overall art direction remain custom MA'KYREN work.

## Integration rule
Do not replace the current game with another blockout. Existing gameplay systems remain intact. Add a separate asset layer and migrate district-by-district.

## Target preview
First production preview should contain one convincing street segment with:
1. Ma'Kyren
2. one detailed vehicle
3. commercial building frontage
4. road/sidewalk system
5. street furniture
6. PBR/material treatment
7. daylight lighting and shadows
8. mobile LOD strategy

Only after that preview is visibly credible should the full city be migrated.
