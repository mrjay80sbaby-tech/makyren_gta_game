# Facade Texture Verification

The high-tier Visual-004 capture after the procedural facade texture pass shows substantially more surface variation than the earlier flat building blocks. Facades now have a dark panel grid with repeated inset surface details, while the separate blue emissive window panels, neon storefronts, traffic vehicles, trees, crosswalk, street lamps, and mission HUD remain readable.

This remains a procedural material pass rather than a final PBR asset replacement. It is intended to make the vertical slice judgeable and to provide a stable fallback while verified CC0/PBR content is introduced through the optional asset loader.
