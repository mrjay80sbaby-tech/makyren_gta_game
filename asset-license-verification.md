# Asset License Verification

Browser verification on August 15, 2026 confirmed:

- Poly Haven’s license page states that its HDRIs, textures, and 3D models are licensed as CC0/public domain, may be used for commercial work, and do not require attribution, although attribution is appreciated. Source: https://polyhaven.com/license
- Kenney’s support page states that assets on its asset pages are public-domain CC0, may be used in commercial projects, and do not require attribution. Kenney also says its logo is reserved for official projects and must not be used as a game asset. Source: https://kenney.nl/support

The repository will continue to require individual asset-page verification and a source record before importing content. Procedural fallbacks remain mandatory for builds that do not include optional assets.
