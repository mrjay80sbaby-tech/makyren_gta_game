# Free Asset Sources for Ma’Kyren

**Author:** Manus AI

The project will prefer **CC0/public-domain assets** and will retain procedural fallbacks so the game remains buildable without external downloads. Every imported asset should be recorded with its source page, asset identifier, license, download date, and any required attribution.

| Source | Suitable content | License position | Production use |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license) | HDRIs, PBR textures, and 3D models | Poly Haven states that its assets are CC0 and may be used for commercial purposes without required attribution.[1] | Environment lighting, asphalt/concrete materials, facade textures, and selected props. |
| [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/support) | Stylized props, vehicles, environment kits, UI assets | Kenney states that assets on its asset pages are public-domain CC0 and may be used commercially without required attribution.[2] | Low-poly mobile fallback props, interaction icons, traffic and street dressing. |
| [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/list) | PBR materials, HDRIs, decals, atlases, and models | Verify the license metadata on each individual asset page before import. The catalog includes road, asphalt, concrete, facade, metal, HDRI, and foliage categories.[3] | Material replacement candidates and decals, subject to per-asset verification. |

## Import policy

No asset should be downloaded into the repository solely because a catalog page lists it as free. The individual asset page must be checked, the license evidence must be recorded, and the asset should be converted to a web-appropriate resolution and format. High-tier assets may use 2K textures, while medium and mobile tiers should use reduced textures, simplified meshes, or procedural fallbacks.
