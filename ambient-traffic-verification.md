# Ambient Traffic Verification

The Visual-004 high tier now renders five lightweight ambient vehicles in the street, while the mobile tier uses two. The high-tier capture shows red and blue traffic vehicles distributed along the road, while the playable blue vehicle, street trees, windows, neon signs, street lamps, and mission HUD remain visible.

The traffic entities update along the road and wrap at the authored street bounds. The scene remains a procedural vertical slice and will eventually need modeled vehicles with wheels, lights, materials, and collision-aware traffic behavior.
